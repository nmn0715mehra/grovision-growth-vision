# Grovision Brand Foundation

Build the complete GROVISION website exactly according to the attached brief below. Treat this as a production-quality brand foundation, not a generic freelancer template. Use the Lovable default modern TypeScript/Tailwind/shadcn architecture, with clean reusable components and responsive behavior. Do not invent client work, testimonials, statistics, social URLs, or experience claims.

BRAND: GROVISION — GROWTH + VISION. Tagline: “WHERE VISION MEETS GROWTH.” Category: digital marketing / digital marketing freelancing / future digital marketing agency. Positioning: an emerging premium Indian digital marketing brand that helps ambitious businesses turn vision into clear direction through strategy, creativity and execution. Personality: premium, intelligent, creative, professional, trustworthy, friendly, strategic, modern, confident.

CORE MESSAGE: “GROVISION helps businesses turn their vision into a clear direction for growth through digital marketing, strategy, creativity and execution.” Belief: businesses can have strong ideas/products and willingness to grow but lack the right direction. Core philosophy: strategy before execution; creativity with purpose; marketing connected to business goals; long-term growth over short-term hype; client success over simply selling services.

AUDIENCE: Indian businesses initially; future US, UK and Europe. Industries: e-commerce, fashion, footwear, fitness, restaurants/cafes, local businesses, startups, personal brands, coaches/consultants, technology and education. Ideal client: ambitious business owner who wants growth but needs digital direction.

LOGO: Use the provided GROVISION logo assets EXACTLY as supplied. Never redraw, redesign, reinterpret, distort, recolor inappropriately, replace, add effects, or generate an alternative. Use the correct supplied variation for light/dark backgrounds, including main logo, icon-only, horizontal, light-background, dark/inverted and favicon/transparent variants where available. Use icon-only in compact mobile/small UI contexts.

COLORS: Deep Navy #0D2B4A, Warm Gold #C9A66B, Warm Beige #F7EDE1, Ivory #FFFAF5. Beige/ivory are primary backgrounds; navy is main text and strong sections; gold is restrained premium accent. Do not make the site completely beige and do not overuse gold. Create contrast with beige, ivory, navy and gold.

TYPE: Montserrat or similarly premium geometric sans-serif. Strong hierarchy, clean readable body text, generous spacing/letter spacing where useful. No script/decorative/futuristic/overly rounded fonts.

UX/VISUAL: premium modern agency-style, minimal, spacious, elegant, creative, professional, conversion-focused, mobile-first. Strong hierarchy, generous whitespace, refined borders/shadows, sophisticated cards, intentional buttons. Avoid excessive glassmorphism, gradients, rounded cards, clutter, generic stock laptop imagery, or copying recognizable brands. Feel like a young ambitious Indian digital strategy studio with international potential.

NAV: sticky desktop navbar with logo left; Home, About, Services, Portfolio, Case Studies, Contact; primary CTA “Let’s Grow Together” (or “Start a Conversation”). Mobile: icon/logo + hamburger and clean mobile navigation with CTA.

HOME STRUCTURE:
1) HERO: GROVISION; “WHERE VISION MEETS GROWTH.”; headline “Turn Your Vision Into Growth.” Supporting copy: “Strategic digital marketing for ambitious businesses that know where they want to go — but need the right direction to get there.” CTAs “Let’s Grow Together” and “Explore Services”. Use provided logo/icon prominently and an elegant abstract growth/strategy visual in brand colors; no generic stock people. Subtle animation.
2) BRAND INTRO: “Growth needs more than visibility. It needs direction.” Explain vision + strategy + creativity + digital execution + growth.
3) SERVICES: “Digital Marketing With Direction.” Services: Digital Marketing Strategy, Brand Strategy, Growth Strategy, Social Media Management, SEO, Paid Advertising. Each with simple icon, name, concise strategic explanation and optional Learn More interaction. No exaggerated claims.
4) WHO WE HELP: “Built for businesses ready to grow.” Elegant cards/list for the target industries. Phrase them as industries GROVISION is designed to help, not existing clients.
5) APPROACH: “From Vision to Growth.” Four memorable steps: 01 Understand — business, audience, goals, challenges; 02 Strategize — right digital direction; 03 Create — purposeful creative; 04 Grow — measure, learn, optimize, improve. Subtle scroll animation.
6) PORTFOLIO: “Selected Work” / “Creative Concepts”. Since there are no real clients yet, do NOT invent projects/results. Use clearly labelled “Concept Project” / “Sample Work” placeholders for Brand Strategy Concept, Social Media Campaign Concept, SEO Strategy Concept, Paid Advertising Concept, Digital Growth Strategy Concept. Make the CMS-like structure easy to replace later with real work.
7) CASE STUDIES: intentional premium “Case studies coming soon” state with copy: “As GROVISION grows, this space will showcase real strategies, challenges, solutions and measurable outcomes.” Prepare future structure: challenge, objective, strategy, execution, results, learnings.
8) ABOUT: introduce GROVISION as a brand plus the person behind it without making it an overly personal resume. State honestly that GROVISION is currently being built while learning digital marketing, building skills, preparing for internship experience and planning to begin freelancing, with long-term goal of becoming a serious digital marketing business. Do not portray an experienced agency owner.
9) PHILOSOPHY: visually strong section with “We believe every ambitious business deserves a clear direction for growth.” Include “WHERE VISION MEETS GROWTH.”
10) CONTACT: “Ready to give your vision a direction?” Supporting copy: “Tell us about your business, your goals and where you want to go.” Form fields: Name, Business/Brand, Email, Phone/WhatsApp, Industry, What do you need help with?, Message. CTA “Start a Conversation”; optional “Free Consultation”. Include email, WhatsApp, phone, Instagram and LinkedIn UI, but because no official social accounts exist, DO NOT invent usernames or URLs. Build placeholders/config fields that can be connected later. Do not invent email/phone/WhatsApp addresses; make contact values easy to configure.
11) FOOTER: GROVISION logo/icon, tagline, navigation, configurable contact placeholders, social icon placeholders, “© GROVISION [current year]. All rights reserved.”

COPY VOICE: confident not arrogant; professional not boring; creative not childish; friendly not casual; intelligent and clear. Avoid phrases like “10x”, “dominate”, “guaranteed results”, “take your business to the next level”. No lorem ipsum.

ANIMATION: subtle fade-up entrances, gentle logo movement, button microinteractions, card hover elevation, navigation transition, smooth scrolling, selective text reveal, gentle icon/image movement. No bouncing, flashing, distracting parallax or animation everywhere. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.

RESPONSIVE: 320/375/390/430 mobile, 768/1024 tablet, 1280/1440/1920 desktop. Mobile must be redesigned, not simply shrunk. No horizontal scroll, overlaps, unreadable text or inaccessible buttons/forms.

SEO: title “GROVISION | Digital Marketing & Growth Strategy”; natural meta description communicating that GROVISION is a creative and strategic digital marketing brand helping ambitious businesses find direction and grow online. Proper H1-H3 hierarchy, semantic HTML, Open Graph metadata, descriptive alt text, natural keywords: digital marketing, digital marketing freelancer, digital marketing consultant, SEO, social media management, growth strategy, digital marketing India. No keyword stuffing.

PERFORMANCE/ACCESSIBILITY: optimize images/fonts/animations, avoid unnecessary libraries, semantic HTML, keyboard/focus states, adequate contrast, labels, reduced motion, responsive images. Clean reusable components: Navbar, Buttons, SectionHeading, ServiceCard, IndustryCard, ProcessSteps, PortfolioCard, ContactForm, Footer. Architecture must make future blog, resources, newsletter, booking, onboarding, packages/pricing, international clients and full agency capabilities easy to add without rebuilding.

IMPORTANT: Build the site as a brand, not merely a website. It should feel established in brand quality while being honest about its current stage. Every section should guide the visitor through: “GROVISION understands business growth” → “creative and professional” → “clear approach” → “I see how they could help” → “I should get in touch.”

Before finishing, self-check all routes/anchors, buttons, forms, responsive breakpoints, logo usage, colors, typography, accessibility, SEO, no fake information, no fake social links, no broken links, no placeholder lorem ipsum, no horizontal scrolling. Make the final UI polished and production-ready.
